Cookie policy

Which cookies the website uses and how to manage them

This page explains which cookies the Disco Pleasure website uses. In short: the website uses no profiling or advertising cookies, only two small technical items stored in your browser.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser to remember some information between visits.

Which cookies we use

NameTypePurposeDuration
dp_lang_hintTechnical (browser storage)Remembers whether you chose a language or closed the invitation to switch, so it does not appear again.Until you delete it
dp_meteoTechnical (browser storage)Keeps the latest weather forecast, so it can be shown even when the weather service does not respond.Until you delete it

The website sets no cookies. The two technical items above stay in your browser, are never sent to us and, by law, do not require your consent.

Third-party cookies

We do not use analytics, advertising or profiling tools. The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts and the weather from Open-Meteo: these services receive your IP address in order to deliver the content, as explained in the Privacy policy.

If you open external links such as Eventbrite, WhatsApp or the maps, those services may use their own cookies according to their policies.

How to manage cookies

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Arc and others). Blocking technical cookies does not prevent you from browsing the website.

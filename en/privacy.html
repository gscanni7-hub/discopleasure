3. Why we process it and on what basis

  • To reply to you and manage your table request: legal basis, your request (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To send you news about dates, tables and events, by email or messaging apps, only if you ticked the box in the form: legal basis, your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To run and protect the website: legal basis, our legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

4. How long we keep it

Request and booking data are kept for as long as needed to manage them, and in any case no longer than the end of the season, unless the law requires otherwise. Technical browsing data are kept only as long as strictly necessary for the operation and security of the service.

5. Who can see the data

Data are never sold or transferred. They may be processed on our behalf, and only for the purposes above, by the providers who help us run the website:

  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting; receives the Tables form data to forward it by email.
  • Resend (resend.com): sends the Tables form emails, to our team and as a confirmation of receipt to the person making the request.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): as an alternative to Resend, delivers the Tables form requests to our team by email.

Some providers are based outside the European Union. In these cases data are transferred with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Linked external services

  • Google Fonts: to display the website's typefaces, your browser downloads them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Open-Meteo and MET Norway: to show the Roccaraso weather, your browser queries Open-Meteo and, if it does not respond, MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). Both receive your IP address.
  • Eventbrite, WhatsApp and Google Maps: reached through links. When you open them you leave our website and their own privacy notices apply.

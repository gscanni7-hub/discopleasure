FAQ

How do I book a table?

Through the table request form: tell us the date and how many you are. We will get back to you privately with the confirmation.

When is the best time to arrive?

Doors open at noon. For a good spot at sunset, come up by 3 pm.

How do I get there?

On skis from the Valle delle Gravare slope, or by car up to Aremogna. All the details on the Getting here page.

What if it snows?

The day's forecast is above. Any change of hours is posted in the News and on social media the same morning.

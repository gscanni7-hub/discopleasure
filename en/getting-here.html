Getting here

Chalet Valentino, Viale delle Gravare snc, 67037 Roccaraso (AQ), Aremogna area, at 1,567 metres.

GPS coordinates: 41.835, 14.055.

Open directions in Google Maps

The most beautiful entrance, and the quickest. The chalet sits at the bottom of the Valle delle Gravare slope, served by the Gravare three-seater chairlift and linked to Pizzalto by the Pizzalto-Gravare gondola.

At the end of the day just head down the valley: the last run drops you right in front of the square. Unclip your bindings, and you have arrived.

From Roccaraso drive up towards Aremogna along via Aremogna. Alternatively, from the SS17 at km 136 take the Barranea municipal road and continue for four to six kilometres.

Leave the car at the Gravare car park or in the Aremogna car parks, then walk to the chalet. At weekends and over the holidays spaces fill up quickly: come up early.

The chalet can also be reached on foot, on packed snow. You need shoes with a sole that grips, ideally snow boots: up here, style starts with your feet.

At sunset the ground freezes. If you walk back down, take it slowly and keep your phone charged.

The forecast for Chalet Valentino, at 1,567 metres, hour by hour and for the coming days. At this altitude the sky changes its mind quickly: check it before you leave, not the night before.

From noon to 7 pm, from the first sun until dark. Around 4:40 pm the sun drops behind the ridge and the lights come on: the moment not to miss.

We open with the Immaculate Conception long weekend, December 5 to 8. Then every day from December 26 to January 10, and every weekend until the closing on March 14. All the dates are in the calendar.

We close at 7 pm, after dark. Plan your way back before you come up, especially if you are a group travelling in several cars.

And if you have been drinking, leave the wheel to someone else. It is the most elegant rule of the evening: we want to see you again next weekend.
